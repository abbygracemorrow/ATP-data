Surface breakdown

What: the share of matches in this view played on each surface. How: counted directly from the CSV's Surface column for every match in the selected year(s) — no surface is invented or excluded. Why: shows the tour's surface mix, and how the selected years compare to the full 2000–2026 period.

Upset rate by ranking gap

What: how often the lower-ranked player beats the higher-ranked (favored) player, grouped by the favorite's own ranking tier. How: for every match with a listed ATP ranking for both players, the better-ranked player is the favorite; an upset is a match the favorite lost. Rate = upsets ÷ matches with valid rankings in that tier. Why: shows whether upsets are more common against lower-ranked favorites than against the very best players.

Tournament spotlight

What: the strongest performer at one tournament, by win rate. How: computed only from matches recorded under the selected tournament name, within the selected year(s). Why: reveals who has actually dominated a specific event, rather than the tour as a whole.
